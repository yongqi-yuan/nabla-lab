Our research

Research interests across fluid mechanics, computation, marine engineering and low-altitude applications.

Research themes

FLOW & COMPUTATION

Fluid Mechanics & Computation ↗

Our interests include computational fluid dynamics, theoretical analysis and physics-informed neural networks for studying fluid motion.

WAVES, STRUCTURES & OCEANS

Waves, Structures & Marine Engineering ↗

Our interests include wave loading, gap resonance, fluid–structure interaction, structural mechanics and offshore renewable energy.

AERODYNAMICS & ACOUSTICS

Aerodynamics & Noise ↗

This work concerns UAV aerodynamics and noise reduction, drawing on fluid mechanics and aeroacoustics.

LOW-ALTITUDE APPLICATIONS

Low-altitude Economy ↗

Our interest in low-altitude aviation focuses on engineering questions related to UAV aerodynamics and noise.

These themes reflect our members’ research and academic interests. Explore the individual profiles for their backgrounds and focus.
