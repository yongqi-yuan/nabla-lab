Fluid resonance in the narrow gap between side-by-side boxes with different corner configurations (opens in a new tab)

Yongqi Yuan, Jiahe Tian, Yu Zhao

Ocean Engineering 353, Part 2, Article 124671 (2026)

Published online 2 March 2026
Issue date 30 April 2026

DOI: 10.1016/j.oceaneng.2026.124671 ↗ (opens in a new tab)View at publisher ↗ (opens in a new tab)Download BibTeX ↓

Exploring gap resonance.

Using a two-dimensional viscous-flow model in OpenFOAM, the study examines fluid resonance between two fixed boxes with sharp, chamfered or rounded corners. It explores how corner shape, corner dimensions and incoming wave amplitude affect resonance and nonlinear wave responses.

Smoothing the corners shifts resonance towards higher frequencies and increases the resonant wave response. The study connects these changes to flow separation, viscous dissipation and higher-harmonic contributions.
