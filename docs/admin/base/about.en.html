About us

WHO WE ARE

An independent
academic community.

Nabla Lab began as a student-led group at Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai in 2025.

Our early activities centred on research and research training. Established on 1 March 2025, the group brought a shared interest in CFD and wave–structure interaction into a common space for learning and investigation.

Today, our members pursue studies and research at different institutions. Nabla Lab provides a continuing connection through which we work on shared research questions.

OUR NAME

The name Nabla.

Nabla Lab takes its name from ∇ (nabla), the differential operator that appears in the Navier–Stokes equations describing fluid motion.

The name reflects our shared interest in fluid mechanics.

SHARED UNDERGRADUATE BACKGROUND

Two institutions,
a common beginning.

Our members have studied, or are studying, in the joint undergraduate programme between Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai and the University of Strathclyde.

Our current academic roles and institutions are listed in each member’s profile.
