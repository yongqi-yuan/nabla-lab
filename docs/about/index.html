About us

An open, independent, and curiosity-driven research community.

Nabla Lab is an open, independent, and curiosity-driven research community.

We share a passion for science and engineering, value collaboration, and embrace the opportunities and challenges of the AI era.

Founded in 2025, Nabla Lab brings together members who now study and work at different universities. We continue to collaborate on research in fluid mechanics, computational fluid dynamics and wave–structure interaction, alongside interests in marine engineering, aerodynamics and noise.

Our main focus is sustained collaborative research. We also share research experience, discuss questions as they arise, and learn from one another. Nabla Editorial provides a space for members to reflect on research, technology and everyday life.
