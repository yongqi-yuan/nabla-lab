ESTABLISHED 2025

Our history

Research, research training and the people who brought Nabla Lab together.

Nabla Lab was established on 1 March 2025. Our story began with earlier CFD work, and continues through shared research interests and academic connections.

2023–2024

Preparatory CFD work

Wave-generation and validation work in ANSYS Fluent prepared the foundation for later studies.

9 December 2024

OpenFOAM environment

An OpenFOAM and Ubuntu environment was established.

February 2025

First OpenFOAM case

The first waves2Foam/OpenFOAM case was set up on 26 February; the toolbox was loaded on 28 February.

1 March 2025

Nabla Lab established

Nabla Lab was established as a student-led group; Yu Zhao was invited to join.

1 April 2025

Gap-resonance study

The gap-resonance study began.

4 June 2025

Key technical progress

Progress was made on key techniques for the study.

27 June 2025

Zhuoxi Hu invited to join

Zhuoxi Hu was invited to join Nabla Lab.

7 August 2025

Main CFD work completed

The main CFD work for the gap-resonance study was completed.

14 August–8 November 2025

Analysis, figures and writing

Results were revisited, with data analysis, figure preparation and writing continuing through early November.

November–December 2025

Writing and revision

Manuscript writing and revisions continued, with completion on 14 December.

24 November 2025

Zhirui Ju invited to join

Zhirui Ju was invited to join Nabla Lab.

19 December 2025

Submitted to Ocean Engineering

A manuscript was submitted to Ocean Engineering.

2 March 2026

Published in Ocean Engineering

The gap-resonance paper by Yongqi Yuan, Jiahe Tian and Yu Zhao was published online as the version of record in Ocean Engineering.
