ABOUT US

A shared beginning,
many academic paths.

Nabla Lab is an independent academic community founded in 2025 by a student-led group at Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai.

Our members share a joint undergraduate background with the University of Strathclyde. As our academic paths lead to different institutions, we keep working together on research questions and shared projects, while supporting research training and exchanging experience.

OUR RESEARCH

Connected interests.

Explore all themes ↗
01
FLOW & COMPUTATION

Fluid Mechanics & Computation ↗

Understanding fluid motion through mechanics and computational approaches. Our interests include computational fluid dynamics and physics-informed neural networks as ways to study fluid behaviour.

02
WAVES, STRUCTURES & OCEANS

Waves, Structures & Marine Engineering ↗

Exploring how waves, fluids and structures interact in marine settings. This theme brings together interests in wave–structure interaction, fluid–structure interaction, structural mechanics, marine engineering and offshore renewable energy.

03
AERODYNAMICS & ACOUSTICS

Aerodynamics & Noise ↗

Studying aerodynamic behaviour and noise associated with unmanned aerial vehicles. Current interests connect fluid mechanics and aerodynamics with the question of UAV noise reduction.

04
LOW-ALTITUDE APPLICATIONS

Low-altitude Economy ↗

An area of interest connecting low-altitude aviation with engineering questions. Within this theme, our current interests include the aerodynamics and noise of unmanned aerial vehicles.
