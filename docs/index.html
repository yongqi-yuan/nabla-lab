About Nabla Lab

∇

Nabla Lab began in 2025 with students from the joint undergraduate programme between Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai and the University of Strathclyde. We now study and work at different universities, and continue to collaborate on research.

Our interests include fluid mechanics, wave–structure interaction, computational fluid dynamics, aerodynamics and noise. We also share methods and experience from our research.
