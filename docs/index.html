About Nabla Lab

Nabla Lab is an open, independent, and curiosity-driven research community. We share a passion for science and engineering, value collaboration, and embrace the opportunities and challenges of the AI era.

Founded in 2025, we continue to collaborate across universities on fluid mechanics, computational fluid dynamics and wave–structure interaction, alongside interests in marine engineering, aerodynamics and noise. We also share research methods and experience.
