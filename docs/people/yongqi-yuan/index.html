01 / ABOUT

About

I am a graduate student in the Department of Engineering Science and at Lady Margaret Hall, University of Oxford.

I am studying towards a DPhil in Engineering Science. My current academic status is Probationary Research Student (PRS).

My research focuses on nonlinear wave forces and wave–structure interaction, combining computational fluid dynamics with theoretical analysis.

I am a co-founder and member of Nabla Lab, where I continue collaborative research with members based at different institutions.
