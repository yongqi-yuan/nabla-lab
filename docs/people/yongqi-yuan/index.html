01 / ABOUT

About

I’m Yongqi Yuan (袁泳棋), a DPhil student in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford.

My current academic status is Probationary Research Student (PRS).

I am a member of Thomas Adcock’s research group (opens in a new tab) and affiliated with Lady Margaret Hall. My research interests include fluid mechanics, wave–structure interaction and computational fluid dynamics.

03 / RESEARCH

Research interests

My research focuses on fluid mechanics, nonlinear wave forces and wave–structure interaction, using CFD and theoretical analysis.

At Oxford, my work concerns wave loading within a research group that studies ocean hydrodynamics and the interaction between waves and infrastructure.
