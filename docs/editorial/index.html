FOUNDING NOTE

An Exciting and Unsettling Age

Nabla Editorial begins as a record of how young researchers experience a changing world. Emerging from informal group discussions, this series explores research, technology, work and everyday life, with particular attention to the opportunities and uncertainties of artificial intelligence. It aims to preserve questions, disagreements and evolving perspectives, rather than predict the future.

About this series

Signed contributions

All Nabla members may contribute signed articles. Each piece expresses its author’s perspective, without requiring a team consensus.

Publication and correction records

Original arguments and first publication dates are preserved. Later perspectives appear in new essays or dated annotations and postscripts; corrections remain documented.

Quotations and feedback

Members’ views are quoted with consent and, where appropriate, anonymised by agreement. Reader feedback is currently welcomed by email.

Feedback by email

Questions, differing views and feedback are welcome by email. Correspondence will not be quoted with attribution without consent.
